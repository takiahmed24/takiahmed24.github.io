Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · Applies to Last Charge (Google Play, app ID com.takiapps.lastcharge)

In short:

  • No sign-up. We never ask for your email or phone number.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone. We do not build in analytics tools of our own.
  • Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. If you do, Google keeps your scores and a backup of your progress.
  • Our own weekly leaderboard shows your player name and run results to other players.
  • Ads are optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob: you only see one when you tap "Watch ad".
  • If you buy shards, Google handles the payment. We never sell your data.

Who we are

Last Charge is published on Google Play by Taki Apps and developed by Raydrim ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and how you can control it. Questions: support@takiapps.com.

Data stored on your phone

The game saves the following only on your device. We do not receive it:

  • Game progress: coins, shards, upgrades, unlocked Wardens, story progress, missions, achievements and records.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, graphics quality and similar options.
  • A small performance log (for example, run length and frame rate, plus your phone model) that helps with troubleshooting. It stays on your phone and is never uploaded.

This data is removed when you use Settings → Privacy & data → Reset progress in the game, clear the app's data, or uninstall the game.

Google services the game uses

Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you sign in with Play Games, Google stores the following under your Google Account:

  • Your leaderboard scores (for example, best distance and score). Leaderboards are public, so other players can see your Play Games name, avatar and score.
  • A cloud save of your game progress, so you can keep it when you change phones.

Signing in is optional. Without it, everything stays on your phone.

Google Play Billing (only if you buy something)

Shard purchases are processed by Google Play. The game receives a purchase record (what you bought and a purchase token) so it can deliver your shards. We never see your payment details.

Last Charge leaderboard (our own server)

So that every player can appear on the weekly RANKS boards, the game sends the following to our leaderboard server (hosted for us by Supabase, encrypted in transit):

  • A random player key made on your phone (it is not linked to your Google Account; the server stores only a scrambled version of it).
  • Your player name: your Play Games display name, or a nickname you pick on RANKS (otherwise an automatic name like "Warden 1234"). Names are public on the boards.
  • Your run results: distance, score and run time, and for competition runs the number of taps/swipes and where you revived.
  • A Play Integrity check result from Google (whether the game is a genuine Play copy on a genuine device), used only to stop cheating.
  • A scrambled (hashed) version of your network address, used only to limit fake accounts. The address itself is not stored.

Weekly results are kept so the boards and season ranks work. To delete your leaderboard data, see the data deletion page.

Crash and performance statistics

Google Play may give us statistics about crashes and app performance (Android vitals) if you allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics with Google. These reports do not identify you to us.

Advertising

Last Charge shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob: you only see an ad when you choose to tap "Watch ad" (to revive, to double a run's coins, or for one free chest a day). There are no banner or pop-up ads, and no ads in competition runs.

  • To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your network address), ad interactions and diagnostic data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first (Google's consent form). You can change your choice any time in the game: Settings → Privacy & data → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads), which also limits ad personalisation.
  • Ad content is limited to a teen-appropriate rating.

How we use and share information

  • We use the information above only to run the game: saving progress, showing leaderboards, stopping cheating, delivering purchases and showing the ads you choose to watch.
  • We do not sell your personal information or share it with data brokers.
  • Information is shared only with the services listed above (Google Play services, Google AdMob, and Supabase, which hosts our leaderboard server).
  • Information sent between the game and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Children

Last Charge is made for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset progress in the game (Settings → Privacy & data) or uninstall it to remove data from your phone.
  • Play Games data (scores, cloud saves) can be deleted in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account.
  • Leaderboard data on our server (player name, run results): email us from the data deletion page and we delete it.
  • Ads: change your consent in Settings → Privacy & data → Ad privacy choices, or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Any other request, such as access or deletion: email us. Full steps are on the data deletion page.

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us and we will help within 30 days.
