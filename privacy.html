Last Charge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy applies to the Android game Last Charge (package com.takiapps.lastcharge), published on Google Play by Taki Apps and developed by Raydrim ("we", "us"). It explains what information the game collects, why, and how you can control it.

Want your data deleted? See Data deletion below or the data deletion request page.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, shards, upgrades, unlocked runners, settings and run statistics) is saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

Information collected through Google services

Last Charge does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. The game uses the following Google services, which may collect information as described:

  • Google AdMob (rewarded ads) — when you choose to watch an ad (for example, to revive), AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can estimate your general location), your interactions with ads (such as views and taps) and diagnostic information (such as app launch time) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • Google Play Games Services (if you sign in) — your Play Games profile, scores, achievements and cloud-saved progress are stored by Google under your Google Account.
  • Google Play Billing (if you buy shards) — purchases are processed by Google. The game receives a record of what you bought so it can give you the shards, but never your payment details.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with anyone other than the Google services above, which are needed to run these features.

Your choices

  • Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • You can change your ad consent choice at any time from the game's Settings screen.
  • Signing in to Play Games is optional.

Third-party privacy policies

  • Google Privacy Policy (covers Google Play, Play Games Services and Play Billing)
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services (AdMob)

Children

Last Charge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data deletion

  • Data on your device: uninstall the game, or go to Settings → Apps → Last Charge → Storage → Clear data.
  • Play Games data (profile, scores, achievements, cloud saves): delete it in the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account & data) or from your Google Account.
  • Any other request: email ipetrick2002@gmail.com with the subject "Last Charge data deletion". We will reply within 30 days.

Purchase records are kept by Google as required for tax and accounting law, under Google's own retention rules. We do not keep any other data about you after deletion.

Security

Information sent between the game and Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
