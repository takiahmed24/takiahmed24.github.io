Delete your Last Charge data

This page is for Last Charge by Taki Apps on Google Play (app ID com.takiapps.lastcharge, developed by Raydrim). Last Charge has no accounts of its own, so there is no game account to close. Here is how to delete each kind of data.

Request deletion by email

Send an email from any address to the link below with the subject "Last Charge data deletion". You do not need to open or reinstall the game. We will confirm within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

ipetrick2002@gmail.com

Delete it yourself

  1. Progress on your phone: in the game open Settings → Privacy & data → Reset progress (tap twice to confirm), or uninstall Last Charge, or open Android Settings → Apps → Last Charge → Storage → Clear data.
  2. Google Play Games data (only if you use Play Games features — scores, achievements, cloud saves): open the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, or manage it in your Google Account.
  3. Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
