Delete your Last Charge data

Applies to Last Charge (Google Play, app ID com.takiapps.lastcharge)

On this page

Request deletion by email Delete it yourself What is deleted and what is kept

Last Charge has no accounts of its own, so there is no game account to close. Here is how to delete each kind of data.

Request deletion by email

Send an email from any address with the subject "Last Charge data deletion". You do not need to open or reinstall the game. We will confirm within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

ipetrick2002@gmail.com

Delete it yourself

  1. Progress on your phone: in the game, open Settings → Privacy & data → Reset progress (tap twice to confirm). You can also uninstall Last Charge, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Last Charge → Storage → Clear data.
  2. Google Play Games data (scores and cloud saves, only if you signed in): open the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, or manage it in your Google Account.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Game progress on your phoneDeleted immediately when you reset progress, clear data or uninstall.
Play Games scores and cloud savesDeleted by Google when you delete them in Play Games.
Email requestsWe delete any data we hold about you and confirm within 30 days. We keep your email only as long as needed to reply, then delete it.
Purchase recordsKept by Google Play as required by tax and accounting law. We do not keep copies.
