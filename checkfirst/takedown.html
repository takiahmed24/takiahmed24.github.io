Respond to a report or ask for review

Last updated October 22, 2026

If a report or a flag count names your number, account, business or website, you can tell your side. We do not publish or remove reports because of threats or payments. We look at evidence.

What you can ask

  • Respond: add your response, which is shown next to the report.
  • Dispute: say the report is wrong and give proof (receipt, chat, delivery record, ID of your own account). The report is labelled Disputed while we check.
  • Correct: fix a detail (wrong amount, date or number).
  • Remove private details: ask us to hide personal information that should not be public.
  • Legal request: send a court order or a legal notice and the exact report reference.

How to ask

In the app: open the report and use Respond to this report. By email: checkfirst@takiapps.com with the report reference (for example CF-2026-000123), what you want changed, your evidence and how we can reach you. We do not publish your email.

What happens next

  1. We confirm we received it within 3 working days.
  2. A moderator who did not publish the report reviews it when possible.
  3. We may label the report Disputed, add your response, correct it, hide it while we check, or remove it.
  4. We write the decision and reason in the report's timeline. We tell you the result by email within 14 days.

False claims or fake documents may lead to your request being refused and, where the law allows, to being reported to the authorities.
