Delete your account and data

Last updated October 25, 2026

In the app

Open My reports → Settings → Delete account…, type DELETE and confirm. This removes your account right away.

Without the app

Email checkfirst@takiapps.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my CheckFirst account. We may ask you to confirm by replying to a code. We finish within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sign-in email and anonymous ID
  • Reports still waiting or in draft, with their proof files; responses that are not published yet; problems you sent
  • Drafts and photos saved on your phone
  • Your Quick flags (they stop counting)
  • Hashed network address records (they are already deleted after 30 days)

What stays

  • Published reports and published responses stay public, no longer linked to you. If you want one removed, email us with the report reference; we remove reports that break our rules, are shown to be false, or reveal private details.
  • Proof files of published or restricted reports are kept, never shown to the public; only moderators and the admin can see them. A report a moderator already acted on is taken out of public view.
  • Moderator decisions and the audit log stay (they never include your name or email).
  • Anything we must keep to follow the law or protect against abuse, for the time the law requires.
