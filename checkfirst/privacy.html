Last updated October 29, 2026

We do not collect your location, contacts, messages, call history, payment card or bank details, or an advertising ID. The app has no ads and no advertising trackers.

Published reports and flags

A report becomes public as soon as it is sent, labelled Unverified: the number, account or link, the category, the title, what happened and the name as reported (shown as "name as reported, not identity proof"). Its photos are shown too: the copies made in the app, with anything you chose to cover blacked out, without location or camera data. Covering is optional. A moderator or admin may review it later and the label may change, for example to Verified. A report without proof is public too, marked "No proof provided". Public pages never show your sign-in name or email; if you type a name in "Your name on the report", it is shown as "Submitted by …", otherwise "Submitted by a CheckFirst user". Quick flags appear only as a count, and only after several different devices flag the same ID. Reports can be wrong, so we show disputes and corrections next to them. If you want a report about you reviewed, see Respond to a report.

Who processes data for us

We use service providers who process data only to run the app: Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage; region Southeast Asia (Singapore)), Brevo (sign-in emails), Google (Google sign-in and Play Integrity), and, before public launch, Cloudflare R2 (file storage). We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data if required by law or a valid court order in Bangladesh.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Original evidence files are private and not reachable by link. Moderator decisions are written to an audit log that cannot be edited or deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete my account) or at this page.
  • Withdraw a Quick flag in the app (My reports → Flags on this phone).
  • Ask to see, correct or erase your data, or object to its use, by emailing checkfirst@takiapps.com. We reply within 30 days.
  • Published reports after you delete your account: published reports and published responses stay public, no longer linked to you. The proof of a published or restricted report is kept, hidden from the public; only moderators and the admin can see it. A report a moderator already acted on is taken out of public view. A public report that nobody has reviewed yet is deleted with your account. Email us if you want a specific report removed; see the terms for when we can.

Children

CheckFirst is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has used the app, email us and we will delete the data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and show a notice in the app.
