1. What CheckFirst is

CheckFirst is a community tool for looking up reports about phone numbers, accounts, links and similar IDs, and for submitting your own reports. Reports are allegations by users. They are not statements of fact by us, not a finding that anyone committed a crime, and not legal, financial or police advice. A lack of reports does not mean an ID is safe.

2. Who may use it

You must be 18 or older and able to enter a contract where you live.

3. Rules for reports and flags

  • Report only what happened to you or what you can show with real evidence.
  • Do not make false, malicious or revenge reports. Do not report people because of who they are.
  • Do not include other people's private details (home address, ID numbers, children's names, faces) that are not needed to show the problem. Hide them with the in-app tool.
  • Do not flag or report an ID you have no real experience with, and do not flag the same ID repeatedly or use many accounts or devices to move a count.
  • Do not upload illegal content, sexual content, malware or anything that threatens or harasses.
