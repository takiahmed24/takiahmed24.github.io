How CheckFirst works

Last updated October 22, 2026

What a search shows

An exact match means a report names the same normalised ID (for example the same phone number after removing spaces and country code differences). Similar names or numbers are shown separately and never under exact matches. If the search service is down, the app says "Search unavailable", never "no reports".

Status labels

LabelMeaning
Supports the reported allegationAt least one piece of evidence a reviewer saw shows the ID in the situation described. It is still an allegation, not a court finding.
No evidence yetA report exists but no evidence has been added or accepted. This label never appears as "supported".
DisputedThe named side or another reviewer challenged the report. Both sides are shown.
Under reviewA moderator put the ID or report on hold. Counts are hidden while it is checked.

How reports are reviewed

  1. The reporter writes what happened and adds proof, after hiding private details on their own phone.
  2. A moderator checks that the proof shows the ID in question, that private details are hidden, and that the wording states what happened without calling anyone a criminal.
  3. The moderator decides: publish, ask for more, reject, or hold. The decision and reason are saved in an audit log that cannot be edited or deleted.
  4. After publishing, anyone can flag a problem; the named side can respond; corrections are added to a visible timeline.

Quick flag

Quick flag is a fast way to say an ID gave you a problem without a full report. It has no free text. A flag count is shown publicly only when several different devices have flagged the same ID, one person's repeated flags never add up, and the count disappears while a report or ID is under review. A count is not proof.

What we do not do

We do not call anyone a "scammer" or "fraudster" in the app's own words. We do not accept payment to add, remove or change a report. We do not publish original screenshots, only the privacy-hidden copy. We cannot recover money.

Moderators

Reviews are done by the publisher's team, currently about 3 hours a week. If reports arrive faster than we can review them, we pause new submissions rather than skip reviews.
