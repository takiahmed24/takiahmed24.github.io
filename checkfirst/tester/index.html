Test CheckFirst on your phone

Four short steps. Use the same Google account (Gmail) for all of them.

  1. Open this page in Chrome

    Opened it from WhatsApp, Messenger or another app? Tap this first, so Google knows it's you. Already in Chrome? Go to step 2.

    Open in Chrome

  2. Join the testers group

    On the Google page tap Join group, then come back here. Already in the Taki Apps testers group? Go to step 3.

    Join the group

  3. Become a tester

    On Google Play tap Become a tester, then come back here.

    Become a tester

  4. Install the app

    Opens CheckFirst in the Play Store app. Tap Install.

    Open Play Store

Play says “not available” or “can't download”? Check that the picture at the top right of the Play Store is the same Gmail you used to join the group and become a tester. Then wait a few minutes and try again.

Please stay a tester for at least 14 days: keep the app installed and don't leave the group. Google needs that before the app can go public.

Not on Android, or a button does nothing? Web links: testers group · become a tester · Play Store page.
