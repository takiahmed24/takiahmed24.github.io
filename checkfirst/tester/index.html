CheckFirst

Test CheckFirst on your phone

Four short steps. Use the same Google account (Gmail) for all of them.

  1. 1Open this page in Chrome

    Opened it from WhatsApp, Messenger or another app? Tap this first, so Google knows it's you. Already in Chrome? Go to step 2.

    Open in Chrome

  2. 2Join the testers group

    On the Google page tap Join group, then come back here. Already in the Taki Apps testers group? Go to step 3.

    Join the group

  3. 3Become a tester

    On Google Play tap Become a tester, then come back here.

    Become a tester

  4. 4Install the app

    Opens CheckFirst in the Play Store app. Tap Install.

    Open Play Store

Play says "not available" or "can't download"? Check the picture at the top right of the Play Store is the same Gmail you used in steps 2 and 3. Then wait a few minutes after step 3 and try again.

Please stay in the test for at least 14 days: keep the app installed and don't leave the group.

Not on Android, or a button does nothing? Use the plain links: testers group · become a tester · Play Store page. Testing more Taki Apps apps? All apps in testing. About the app: CheckFirst pages · checkfirst@takiapps.com
