Contact us

Found a bug, have a question or want to share feedback? Email support@takiapps.com and include:

  • Your phone model (for example, Samsung Galaxy A14)
  • The game version (Google Play → Last Charge → About this game)
  • What happened, and a screenshot or screen recording if you can

We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Not if you sign in with Google Play Games. Your progress is backed up to your Google Account, and it comes back when you sign in on the new phone. Without signing in, progress is saved only on the old phone.

Play Games sign-in doesn't work.

Make sure the Google Play Games app is installed and up to date, then restart the game. Sign-in only works in the version installed from Google Play.

The game runs slowly on my phone.

Open Settings in the game and lower the graphics quality. Closing other apps and turning off battery-saver mode also helps.

I bought shards but didn't get them.

Restart the game while connected to the internet. Pending purchases are delivered automatically. If they still don't arrive, email us with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your purchase receipt email).

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund through the Google Play refund policy.

How do I delete my data?

See the data deletion page for step-by-step instructions, or email us with the subject "Last Charge data deletion".
