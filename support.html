Common questions

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?
No, if you're signed in with Google Play Games, your progress is tied to your Google Account and carries over.

Can I get a refund for a purchase?
Purchases are handled by Google Play. Refund requests should go through the Google Play refund policy.

Can I request deletion of my data?
Yes — see the data deletion page for step-by-step instructions, or email us at the address above with the subject "Last Charge data deletion".
